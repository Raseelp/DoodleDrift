Privacy Policy

Finding Light (package: dev.raseel.wake)
Last updated: 21 November 2026

Finding Light is a calm boat game: you steer across still lakes and close loops with your trail to light lanterns before dark. It works entirely on your device, and it does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no crash-reporting service. The developer does not receive any data from your device.

Your progress

The game saves your progress, your best times and your settings (such as sound) in the app's own private storage on your phone, so you can carry on where you left off. This file never leaves your device. It is deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Internet access

The game does not request the internet permission and does not connect to any server. It plays fully offline.

Permissions the game uses

  • Vibration: for small haptic bumps during play.

Third-party software

The game is built with the open-source Godot Engine and uses an open-source font and freely licensed 3D models and icons, all bundled inside the app. They run locally on your device and none of them send data anywhere. Credits are listed inside the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever adds something that changes this policy (for example ads), the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
